How CreditFair Works

The backend modeling engine leverages statistical patterns in historical credit underwriting data to predict default probabilities.

01

Trained on historical Lending Club accepted loan records containing real consumer credit outcomes.

02

19 Features

Considers loan metrics, credit lines, DTI, FICO, public records, and homeownership details.

03

Built using supervised Binary Classification algorithms mapping historical loans to outcome classes.

04

Establishes if a borrower will successfully pay in full or eventually charge off.

What Does CreditFair Analyze?

A closer look at some of the key indicators selected from the 19 input features that carry the highest statistical weight in our predictor.

▲

The cost of borrowing, which reflects baseline market risk assessments.

Industry-standard credit rating showing historical trustworthiness.

The percentage of revolving credit limits currently being drawn.

🕑

Duration of active credit files and total available credit accounts.

About CreditFair

CreditFair is designed as an educational machine learning project demonstrating how supervised learning can be applied to historical loan data to identify potential default risk.

By extracting insights from historical borrower profiles and repayment patterns, this dashboard serves as an interactive showcase of predictive analytics in risk scoring and fintech automation.
