How ClaimWise Works

A structured six-step workflow designed to transform raw insurance records into ML-ready features.

01

DATA

Ingests 50,000 raw insurance claims records.

02

CLEAN

Drops id column and handles missing values.

03

ENCODE

Applies LabelEncoder to text categories.

04

SCALE

Normalizes continuous variables via StandardScaler.

05

TRAIN

Fits regression algorithms on 40,000 training rows.

06

PREDICT

Estimates continuous insurance claim loss values.
